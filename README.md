## 🌍 WALL·E – My Favorite Movie  

There’s something hauntingly peaceful about *WALL·E*. It’s not just a love story between two robots — it’s a quiet reminder of what humanity loses when it forgets to care. I love how the movie balances loneliness, curiosity, and hope without saying much. The silence speaks louder than words.  

WALL·E’s fascination with the smallest human things — a lighter, a plant, a song — hits differently. It’s like watching innocence rediscover the world. Maybe that’s why I like it so much — it feels both lonely and alive at the same time.
